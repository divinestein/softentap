About SoftenTap

SoftenTap is a how-to site about everyday water problems at home: hardness, scale, softeners, salt-free conditioners, under-sink reverse osmosis, and replacement filters. The site is at softentap.com.

What you can expect

  • Guides you can use to read a utility report and decide whether a device matches the job you actually have.
  • Figures taken from cited public sources, with the year of the data called out when it matters.
  • No claims that we tested a product, ranked a “best” list from lab work, or measured scale in a house.

A local worked example

Several guides use WaterOne, the public water utility that serves Leawood and many other cities in Johnson County, Kansas, as a worked example. We use it because the utility publishes clear hardness numbers and conversion advice, not because this site is affiliated with WaterOne. It is not.

Who writes this

Guides are published by SoftenTap editors. Questions about the project can go on the contact page, which is still a placeholder until a public inbox is opened.
