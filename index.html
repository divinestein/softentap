How to use this site

Number first, problem second, device last

  1. Find your number

    Pull total hardness from your utility’s latest report and convert it to grains per gallon. On a private well, get a lab test instead.

  2. Name the real problem

    Scale, soap and spotting, chlorine taste, or a specific contaminant. Each one points to a different kind of device.

  3. Match the device to the job

    Check the certification that fits the claim (NSF/ANSI 44, 42, 53, 401 or 58) before you compare brands or prices.
