Affiliate disclosure

How SoftenTap makes money, and how we keep that separate from what we write.

SoftenTap is an independent how-to site about home water treatment. Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and later buy something, we may receive a commission. That does not change the price you pay.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

As of November 18, 2026, SoftenTap is an affiliate partner of Waterdrop. As of November 20, 2026, SoftenTap is also an affiliate partner of Epic Water Filters. If you click a marked Waterdrop or Epic Water Filters link and later buy something, we may receive a commission. Other links labeled as placeholders — for example, a link that points to #affiliate-aquasana — do not go to a merchant and do not pay us. They mark where a real affiliate link may be added later.

When a live affiliate link is added, it will be marked clearly next to the link or recommendation (for example, with “affiliate link” or “paid link”) and, for HTML links, will use a sponsored relationship attribute such as rel="sponsored nofollow". A short disclosure will also appear on the same page, near the recommendation — not only in the footer or on this disclosure page.

We have not tested the products mentioned on this site in a lab or in homes. Mentions of brands such as Aquasana, Waterdrop, Clearly Filtered, Epic Water Filters, SpringWell, AquaTru, US Water Systems, iSpring, or Amazon are examples of merchants or programs that exist in this category, not a claim that we ranked or endorsed them from testing.

Editorial judgments — including when we say a device type is a poor fit for a job — will not be written to protect a commission.

This disclosure is meant to follow the Federal Trade Commission’s guidance on endorsements and testimonials, including the requirement to clearly and conspicuously disclose material connections, and Amazon Associates’ identification and link-level disclosure rules. If something on a page is unclear, see the about page. Questions about this disclosure can go to support@softentap.com.
