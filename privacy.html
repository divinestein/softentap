Privacy

Privacy policy

This first version of SoftenTap is a static website. It does not use analytics or advertising tracking scripts, does not set tracking cookies, and does not have user accounts or a working contact form.

What we collect right now

We do not collect personal information through this site in its current form. Your browser will still make a normal request for pages and files. If this site is later hosted on GitHub Pages or another host, that host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested) under its own policy. We do not control those logs.

Affiliate links

Placeholder affiliate links on this site do not send you to a merchant. If we later add live affiliate links, the merchant or affiliate network may set cookies or process your order under their privacy policy. We do not receive your payment-card details.

External links

Guides link to utilities, government agencies, NSF, and university pages. Those sites have their own policies.

Changes

If we add a contact form, newsletter, comments, or measurement tools that collect data, we will update this policy before or at the same time those features go live.
