Privacy

Privacy policy

This version of SoftenTap is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use first-party analytics or advertising tracking scripts, does not set first-party tracking cookies, and does not have user accounts or a working contact form.

What we collect right now

We do not collect personal information through forms or accounts on this site in its current form. Your browser will still make a normal request for pages and files. GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, date and time, and the page requested) under GitHub’s own privacy policy. We do not control those logs.

Fonts, styles and scripts are served from softentap.com itself, so loading a page does not send requests to font or analytics services.

Affiliate links and third parties

Some links on this site are live affiliate links, including marked Waterdrop and Epic Water Filters links. Clicking a live affiliate link will take you to a merchant or an affiliate network (for example Waterdrop, Epic Water Filters, Amazon, or a network such as Rakuten Advertising, CJ Affiliate, impact.com, FlexOffers, Everflow, UpPromote, or PMX). Those companies may set cookies, measure the click, and process any order under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment-card details. Links labeled as placeholders do not send you to a merchant.

If Amazon Associates links are used, Amazon may collect information as described in Amazon’s privacy notice when you visit Amazon after clicking a Special Link.

External links

Guides link to utilities, government agencies, NSF, and university pages. Those sites have their own policies.

Contact

You can reach us at support@softentap.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message, and we use them only to reply to you.

Changes

If we add a contact form, newsletter, comments, analytics, or other tools that collect data, we will update this policy before or at the same time those features go live.
